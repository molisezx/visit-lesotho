Why Lesotho

Small in size, huge in scenery. Everything below is a day's drive from Maseru.

Mountains

The Maloti range holds snow in winter and ski slopes at Afriski.

Water

Rivers carve gorges and feed mega-dams such as Katse.

Culture

Basotho blankets, mokorotlo hats and living village traditions.

Adventure

Pony trekking, hiking, abseiling and 4x4 mountain passes.
