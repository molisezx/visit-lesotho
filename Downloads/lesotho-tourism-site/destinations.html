HighlandsEasy

Katse Dam

One of the highest dams in Africa, part of the Lesotho Highlands Water Project. Take the dam wall tour and a boat trip.

Time needed: Day trip or overnight

SouthernModerate

Maletsunyane Falls

A 192 m waterfall near Semonkong, reached by a short walk to the viewpoint or a gorge hike.

Time needed: Half day

MountainsHard

Sani Pass

A rough 4x4 road linking Lesotho with KwaZulu-Natal, with views over the escarpment.

Time needed: Full day

CultureEasy

Basotho Cultural Heritage

Thatched buildings and an open amphitheatre: a place to experience music, dance and storytelling.

Time needed: 2-3 hours

Thaba-Bosiu
CentralEasy

Thaba-Bosiu

The flat-topped mountain fortress where King Moshoeshoe I founded the Basotho nation. Guided walks tell the story.

Time needed: Half day

Afriski Mountain Resort
MountainsModerate

Afriski Mountain Resort

Southern Africa's ski resort in the Maluti, with snow in winter and mountain biking in summer.

Time needed: 1-3 days

Ts'ehlanyane National Park
MountainsModerate

Ts'ehlanyane National Park

Protected high-altitude wilderness with rare plants, rivers and hiking trails.

Time needed: 1-2 days

Maseru and Craft Markets
CentralEasy

Maseru and Craft Markets

Lesotho's capital: craft centres, mokorotlo hats, woven mats and Basotho blankets.

Time needed: Half day

    Travel tip: mountain weather changes quickly. Check road conditions before driving Sani Pass or any highland route.
