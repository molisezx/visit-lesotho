Activities

Pick your pace: gentle culture, steady hiking or heart-in-mouth adventure.

Easy to Moderate

Pony trekking

The Basotho pony is the traditional way to cross the highlands. Rides run from one hour to multi-day treks.

Where: Semonkong, Malealea

All levels

Hiking

Trails from short waterfall walks to summit attempts on the Maloti peaks.

Where: Ts'ehlanyane, Thaba-Bosiu

Hard

Abseiling

Drop beside Maletsunyane Falls on one of the longest commercial abseils.

Where: Semonkong

Moderate

Skiing and snow

Winter ski slopes and summer mountain biking at Afriski.

Where: Afriski

Hard

4x4 passes

Drive the switchbacks of Sani Pass with an experienced operator.

Where: Sani Pass

Easy

Cultural visits

Village stays, craft workshops and traditional music and dance.

Where: Morija, Maseru
