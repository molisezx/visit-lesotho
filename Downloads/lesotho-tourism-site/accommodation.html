Before you book

Book 2-3 months ahead for June to August (ski season) and for December and Easter holidays. Outside those periods, a few weeks is usually enough.

Warm layers all year (nights at altitude are cold), a waterproof jacket, sturdy shoes, sunscreen and some cash in maloti for rural areas.

Some remote lodges prefer two nights because of long driving distances. Ask when you enquire.

The South African rand is widely accepted alongside the loti, but carry loti for small purchases.
