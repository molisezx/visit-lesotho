About this website

Visit Lesotho is a tourism website that introduces travellers to the Kingdom in the Sky: its mountains, waterfalls, mountain passes and Basotho culture. It lists destinations, places to stay with prices, a photo gallery and a booking form.

The site was designed and developed as a Principles of Web Design project at Limkokwing University of Creative Technology, Lesotho, using HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5 and JavaScript.
